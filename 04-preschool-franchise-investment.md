# Preschool Franchise Investment in India

## Introduction

Investment is one of the most important factors to consider before starting a preschool franchise in India.

The total investment required for a preschool can vary depending on the franchise brand, city, property size, location, infrastructure, classroom requirements, staff strength, and business model.

Entrepreneurs should calculate both the initial setup cost and the recurring operating expenses before making a financial decision.

---
